Rev. Eric Agya Mensah

(General Overseer)



He is the Founder and General overseer of Global Life Chapel (a non-denominational commission), all across the country and beyond. He is popularly called Daddy by his sheep and anybody under his covering.
Called as a prophetic intercessor, his ministry has brought the divine presence and comfort through prophetic directions from the Holy Spirit into the lives of many. He is a firebrand prophet and an apostle with deep insight into the plans and purposes of God for this generation.
He is a man sent by God and approved with miracles, signs and wonders. He has made himself available in massive ways for the body of Christ in these end times. God has used him tremendously in the prophetic office for deliverances and ministration of the Word.
Rev. Eric’s untamed passion for the work of God is evidenced by his consistent efforts to help spread he Gospel of our Lord Jesus according to scriptures by establishing the church and God in rural and less privileged areas just to help them know the life changing power of God the father, the son and the Holy Spirit.
Through his faith-powered messages, he is continuously shaping the destinies of millions across continents and causing them to live the victorious Christian Life. His Vision is to bring hope to the hopeless and empower the Body of Christ in the walk of faith and holiness.
Rev Eric’s passion extends to the younger generation who he believes are God’s sweethearts and must be guided in their walk with Jesus Christ, lest they fall by the wayside.
