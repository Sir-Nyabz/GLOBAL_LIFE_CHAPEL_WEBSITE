The "Feed One Thousand Homeless Project 2021" is a community outreach initiative based on Matthew 25:35-40 launched by Global Life Chapel by the inspiration of the Holy Spirit aimed at providing nutritious meals for homeless individuals during the peak of the Covid-19 pandemic when there was little or no economic activity. The project was designed to alleviate the struggles that homeless individuals face on a daily basis.
The project involved the preparation and distribution of 1000 meals. The meals will be prepared by volunteers from the church, using locally-sourced ingredients, and will be packaged in to-go containers to make them easy for homeless individuals to carry with them.
The project also provided an opportunity for the church to engage with members of the community and to raise awareness about the message of Jesus Christ which is salvation and repentance as was commanded in Mark 16:15, the great commission.
In addition to providing food, the project also offered other services, such as access to hygiene products and clothing donations. Volunteers from the church engaged with the homeless individuals and provided support and resources as needed.
The "Feed One Thousand Homeless Project 2021" was an important initiative that highlighted the church's commitment to serving its community and helping those in need. By providing nutritious meals and other essential resources, the project was aimed at making a positive impact on the lives of homeless individuals and to inspire others to get involved in addressing the issue of food insecurity.
