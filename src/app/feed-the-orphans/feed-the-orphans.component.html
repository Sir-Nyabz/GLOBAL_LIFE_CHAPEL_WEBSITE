FEED THE ORPHANS EVERY WEEK PROJECT

The Feed the Orphans Every Week project is a charitable initiative organized by Global Life Chapel, a church committed to serving the needs of vulnerable children in our community. The project aims to provide nutritious meals to orphans every week by soliciting funds from church members and presenting them to an orphanage home.
Through this project, we aim to make a positive impact on the lives of children who have lost their parents or have been abandoned, and provide them with the basic sustenance they need to grow and thrive. Our goal is to ensure that every child in the orphanage has access to healthy and nutritious meals, and that no child goes hungry.
We believe that this project is a tangible expression of our commitment to serving our community and living out our faith. By coming together as a church community and pooling our resources, we can make a significant difference in the lives of these vulnerable children and provide them with hope for a brighter future.
We invite all members of our church community and anyone who shares our passion for helping others to support this worthy cause. Your contributions will go directly towards feeding the orphans and making a positive impact on their lives. Thank you for your support and generosity!
