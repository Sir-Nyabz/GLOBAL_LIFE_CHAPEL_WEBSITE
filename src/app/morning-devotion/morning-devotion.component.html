Morning Devotion

Start Your Day With Jesus

"Start your day with Jesus" is a non-denominational fellowship that meets in the early morning hours from Monday to Thursday, specifically from 5:00AM to 6:00AM. During this time, members come together to engage in prophetic prayers, seeking to overcome any negative words or prophecies that may have been spoken over them while they were sleeping during the night.
The primary aim of this fellowship is to start the day on a positive note by commanding it through prayer, and to seek guidance and direction from the Holy Spirit. Members strengthen their faith and develop a deeper connection with God.
Through the use of prophetic prayers, we claim victory over any negative words or prophecies that may have been made over their lives while they were sleeping. Based on Psalm 18:3, which speaks of calling upon the Lord and being saved from one's enemies.
While the fellowship is non-denominational, it is open to anyone who wishes to participate, regardless of their background or beliefs. Members come from a diverse range of backgrounds and are united in their desire to seek a deeper connection with God and to start their day in a positive and fruitful manner.
